Rasta ATR/XEX Control

A browser-based converter from pictures to Atari 8-bit mode E images with per-scanline colours, exported as ATR boot disk or XEX.

Inspired by RastaConverter by ilmenit, the original developer. It is a simplified re-implementation (no sprites, no mid-line colour changes), not a port of his code.
