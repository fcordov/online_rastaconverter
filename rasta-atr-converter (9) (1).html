XEX: loads at $2000, keeps up to 4 pictures in RAM and switches between them with a brief blank (it switches BASIC off to use the RAM under it). ATR: bootable disk that keeps the pictures on the disk and loads one at a time while the screen is blank, so it holds many more: about 10 on a 90K disk, 13 on 130K, and the disk grows beyond that as needed. A real floppy drive takes a second or two per picture, emulators and SD-card devices are near-instant. Both loop forever; the interval is counted in video frames, so it is exact on PAL (50 Hz) and NTSC (60 Hz).
